# Texture sources

All five files in this folder are generated by `tools/textures.py` (numpy + Pillow, plus ffmpeg for the video) from fixed seeds, so a re-run reproduces them exactly:

- `graph-paper.jpg`, `halftone-scan.jpg`, `lens-dirt.jpg`, `paper-scan-dark.jpg`: procedural stills.
- `felt-fibre.mp4`: a 62-frame, 12 fps procedural loop (62 frames = 155 frames at 30 fps, the length `FeltFibre` loops).

No third-party material: no scans, photos, stock textures or AI-generated images went into them. They are original work released under the repository's MIT licence, like the rest of the library.

Regenerate with `python tools/textures.py` (see the header of that script for options).
