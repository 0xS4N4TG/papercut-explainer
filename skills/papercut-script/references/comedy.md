# Comedy and format bank

The humour that works in these reels is **situational and visual**. The jokes come from the situation and the characters, and almost never from internet memes. Each gag stands in for a fact the reel would otherwise just state. Write the gags after the script, as a column in the staging table: one per banner.

## Why it works

- The narrator stays deadpan and the picture is silly. That contrast is the joke.
- A gag that explains is both entertaining and teaching, so it never costs time.
- Funny reels get sent to friends, and sends are the strongest signal in the feed.

## 1. Formats (one per reel)

Pick the format whose built-in story matches the topic's tension.

| Format | Built-in story | Fits topics like |
|---|---|---|
| Courtroom | accusation, arguments, verdict | getting an honest opinion, catching a lie, judging an idea |
| Pitch show | pitch, hard questions, "I'm out" | validating ideas, pricing, startups |
| Cooking show | ingredients, steps, taste test | recipes, prompt setups, workflows |
| Heist movie | the target, the crew, the break-in, the getaway | getting paid things free, running big AI at home |
| Nature documentary | a hushed narrator watches a creature in the wild | how an AI "behaves", agents working alone |
| Dating show | contestants, questions, the pick | choosing between tools or models |
| Character-select screen | pick a fighter, see its stats | levels, plans, model tiers |
| Boss fight | the hero vs a giant enemy | bugs, security holes, limits |
| Job interview | candidate, questions, hired or not | delegating tasks to AI |
| Olympic podium / talent show | contestants, scores, medals | rankings, comparisons |
| Hospital / doctor | symptoms, diagnosis, cure | fixing a broken setup |
| Airport security | bags scanned, the suspicious item | privacy, data leaks, safety checks |
| Escape room | locked, clues, out | getting unstuck, hidden settings |
| Infomercial | "Are you tired of...?", product, "But wait, there's more!" | free swaps, tools that replace paid ones |

## 2. Gag types (one per banner)

| Type | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Costume per idea | the mascot keeps its own item and adds one prop that says the idea | judge's wig, a beret for the poetic model, a wizard hat for the big model, QA helmet, burglar beanie, chef's hat |
| Literal pun | the words of the line acted out literally | "bugs" as real beetles; a "Yes Man of the Month" certificate; "cards in cards in cards" zooming forever |
| Number as a prop | the statistic becomes an object that behaves | a message tower growing to 15,000; a price tag cut with scissors; a cash register at $0.00 |
| Struggling character | the mascot has a bad time on the honest-catch line | slumped with "re-check / but... / what if..." bubbles; eyes drooping as $1M flips to $0; frozen in an ice cube while a loading bar crawls |
| Returning prop | a prop from frame 0 changes in the CTA scene | I'M OUT signs become FREE signs; the YES stamp snaps under HONEST; the API bill gets shredded |
| Absurd beat | a silly thing in the background that nobody mentions | cash bursting into a campfire; GPU fans spinning so hard the cap blows off |
| Stamp slam | a rubber stamp delivers a verdict | NOT GUILTY 7-5, HONEST, FREE, PASS |
| Freeze frame | the scene freezes with a label | "yep, that's me." on the struggling beat |

## 3. Memes: at most one per reel

Rebuild the meme in the series' own style. Never paste the real image, clip or audio, because that risks copyright claims and the platform muting the sound. A rebuild that parodies the format is safe and it's yours. It must read in under a second and make the point.

Long-running formats that most viewers know. Check that a meme is still alive before using it:
- **"This is fine"**: the mascot sipping coffee in a burning server room (a broken setup, ignored warnings).
- **Two-panel approval**: no to one thing, yes to another (old way vs new way).
- **Distracted boyfriend**: tempted by the new tool.
- **Galaxy brain**: escalating levels of cleverness (effort levels, prompt tricks).
- **"Is this a pigeon?"**: mislabelling (calling a chatbot an agent).
- **Surprised face**: the obvious consequence.
- **Two buttons / sweating**: a hard choice between options.
- **"Expectation vs reality"**: the promise vs what you actually get.

Region: follow `SERIES.md`. If the audience is global and English-speaking, avoid references that only one country gets (a national sport, a local film industry).

## 4. Don'ts

- A joke in the voiceover. The narrator never jokes.
- More than one meme, or a meme that doesn't explain anything.
- Real faces, logos or TV stills. Even competitors do this, but it's a likeness and copyright risk. Use costumes and props that suggest the show instead.
- A gag that hides the banner, the captions or the key number.
- A gag the viewer has to pause to understand. If it doesn't read in 1 s, cut it.
