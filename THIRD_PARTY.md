# Third-party material

The code, docs, sound effects, music beds and textures in this repository are MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`).
The items below are not ours, or need a note.

## Remotion

The video pipeline runs on [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev). Remotion is source-available under its own licence, not MIT.
It is free for individuals and small teams; companies above its size threshold need a paid licence. Read
<https://www.remotion.dev/license> before using this commercially. Nothing from Remotion is vendored here; `npm install` fetches it.

## Fonts

`library/papercut/public/fonts/` holds woff2 subsets of four open fonts, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The licence texts and copyright lines are in `library/papercut/public/fonts/LICENSES.txt`.

| Font | Use | Project |
|---|---|---|
| Archivo Black | banners | https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/ArchivoBlack |
| Fraunces | caption tags | https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces |
| Inter | UI labels | https://github.com/rsms/inter |
| JetBrains Mono | terminal and code | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |

## Backdrops

`library/papercut/public/bg/backdrops/` has 24 paper-diorama backdrops. Sixteen were made with an AI image model and
then checked and measured by hand (floor line, mood). Eight were drawn in code, and their sources are in
`library/papercut/backdrops-src/`. Some jurisdictions treat purely AI-generated images as not copyrightable. They
contain no logos, text or people, and you can use them freely, but you may not be able to enforce rights in them.

## Textures

The film-look textures in `library/papercut/public/bg/textures/` are generated by `library/papercut/tools/textures.py`.
No third-party texture packs are included.
