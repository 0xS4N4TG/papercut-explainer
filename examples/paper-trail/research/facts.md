## Facts

Captured 2026-05-14 unless a line says otherwise. All invented.

1. **What it is.** Echolark is an open-source desktop app that clones a voice from a short clip and reads text in it. Free to use. Repo tagline: "The open-source, fully-local Vocalyst alternative". Source: https://example.com/echolark/readme. Confirmed.
2. **Who made it.** One developer wrote almost all of it: 94% of commits come from one author. Source: https://example.com/echolark/graphs/contributors. Confirmed.
3. **Stars.** 12,000+. The repo was created 2026-04-22, so about three weeks. Source: https://example.com/echolark (repo header). Confirmed; the number moves, so re-check on the day of posting.
4. **Where it runs.** Desktop installers for macOS, Windows and Linux. It is an app on your computer, not a website. Source: https://example.com/echolark/releases. Confirmed.
5. **Hardware.** No graphics card needed. It runs on a plain CPU, just slower. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/requirements. Confirmed.
6. **Old laptops.** The maintainer's performance page tests it on a ten-year-old laptop. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/performance. Confirmed.
7. **Speed on that laptop.** A 10-minute video dub takes about an hour on the ten-year-old laptop. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/performance. Uncertain: one benchmark by the maintainer, not repeated by anyone else, so the banner says "ABOUT".
8. **Cloning input.** It asks for 5 to 15 seconds of clean audio. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/cloning. Confirmed.
9. **Script screen.** Buttons and menu items: "Import script", "Paste", "Insert". The primary button is "Clone voice", in magenta-pink (#D6428F). Once a voice is cloned, it reads any text back in that voice. The maintainer's demo sentence is "Local cat wins the election". Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/script and screenshot https://example.com/echolark/img/script.png. Confirmed.
10. **Audiobooks.** It turns a whole book into audio in a chosen voice. One preset is "The Librarian: warm UK audiobook narrator". Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/audiobooks. Confirmed.
11. **Dictation.** A floating microphone types what you say into the app you are in. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/dictation. Confirmed.
12. **Transcripts.** The "Upload & Transcribe" panel ("Choose file...") writes out a recording and labels each line "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2". Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/transcribe. Confirmed.
13. **Local models.** Six kinds of model install on your machine: language, translation, speaker labelling, dictation, speech-to-text and voice. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/models. Confirmed.
14. **Dubbing.** Three steps: Upload, Translate, Dub. The dub keeps your cloned voice. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/dubbing. Confirmed.
15. **Languages.** 210 dubbing languages. The "Manage languages" list scrolls through them (Hausa, Māori, Polski, and so on). Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/languages. Confirmed.
16. **Offline translation.** The translation model is listed as "Translate model (Local, Heavy) · Offline". Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/dubbing. Confirmed.
17. **Privacy.** No accounts, no cloud, no API keys. Recordings stay on your machine. Source: https://example.com/echolark/readme#privacy. Confirmed.
18. **Watermark.** Every exported clip carries an AI watermark. The docs call it inaudible. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/safety. Confirmed that the watermark exists; "inaudible" is the docs' claim, not tested here, so the banner says "DOCS:".
19. **Vocalyst free plan.** It cannot clone voices. Source: https://example.com/vocalyst/pricing. Confirmed.
20. **Vocalyst Starter plan.** $15 a month, includes voice cloning. Source: https://example.com/vocalyst/pricing. Confirmed.
21. **The yearly cost.** $15 x 12 = $180 a year. Derived from fact 20.
22. **Vocalyst dubbing.** 40+ languages. Source: https://example.com/vocalyst/dubbing. Confirmed.
23. **Vocalyst is a website.** You clone and dub in the browser, on its servers. Source: https://example.com/vocalyst/privacy. Confirmed.

## Caveats and uncertain items

- **C1.** Fact 7 is one benchmark from the maintainer. The banner says "ABOUT AN HOUR"; the spoken line says only "slower".
- **C2.** The watermark being inaudible is the docs' claim. The banner is worded "DOCS: YOU CAN'T HEAR THE WATERMARK".
- **C3.** The models download on first run (several GB), so the first install needs internet. After that, translation and everything else works offline (facts 13, 16). The reel says "nothing gets uploaded", which is true of the app, but the guide must mention the one-time download. Source: https://example.com/echolark/docs/install.
- **C4.** The star count and the competitor's price change. Re-check facts 3, 20 and 22 on the day of posting.
- **C5.** Voice cloning needs the speaker's consent. The guide says so; the reel does not.
- **C6.** Quality of dubs varies by language (docs/languages). The reel claims 210 languages, not 210 equally good ones.
